More than 40 million people in the United States speak Spanish at home, and in New York it's part of everyday working life. If you collaborate with Spanish-speaking colleagues or clients, a few well-chosen phrases go a long way, long before you're fluent.

## Opening the meeting

1. **"Buenos días a todos. ¿Empezamos?"** Good morning, everyone. Shall we start?
2. **"Gracias por venir."** Thanks for coming.
3. **"El objetivo de hoy es…"** Today's goal is…

## Keeping the conversation moving

4. **"¿Podrías repetirlo, por favor?"** Could you repeat that, please? Nobody minds, and it shows you're listening.
5. **"Si te entiendo bien…"** If I understand you correctly… Perfect for confirming before you respond.
6. **"Buena pregunta."** Good question. It buys you a few seconds to think.
7. **"Estoy de acuerdo, pero…"** I agree, but… A polite way to add a different view.

<Callout title="Tú or usted?">
  In most modern workplaces, "tú" is fine with colleagues. With senior clients or in more formal settings,
  start with "usted" and switch if they do.
</Callout>

## Wrapping up

8. **"Entonces, los próximos pasos son…"** So, the next steps are…
9. **"Te envío un resumen por correo."** I'll send you a summary by email.
10. **"Gracias, que tengan un buen día."** Thanks, have a great day.

## Practice beats perfection

Pick two phrases and use them in your next meeting. Your colleagues will appreciate the effort far more than they'll notice any mistakes.

> Confidence in a language starts with ten phrases you can say without thinking.
